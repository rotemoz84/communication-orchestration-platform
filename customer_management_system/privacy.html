Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Full Name - To address you properly
  • Phone Number - To contact you about your inquiry
  • Email Address - For follow-up communication (optional)
  • Pregnancy Week - Sensitive health information for service provision
  • Service Interest - To understand your needs
  • Technical Data - IP address and browser information for security

How We Use Your Information

Primary Purpose: Lead generation and customer service

Contact Methods: Phone calls and emails to provide services

Service Delivery: Responding to inquiries and scheduling appointments

No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties

Your Rights

Access to Information

You have the right to request access to all information we hold about you.

Correction of Information

You can request correction of any inaccurate information or deletion of your data.

Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information.

Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw your consent for data processing at any time.

Data Security

We protect your information using industry-standard security measures:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption - All communications between your browser and our server are encrypted
  • Access Controls - Limited access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular Security Updates - We maintain up-to-date security protocols
  • Secure Storage - Your data is stored in secure, protected systems

Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary for our business purposes and to comply with legal requirements.

For sensitive health information (pregnancy week), we retain data for up to 3 years from the date of interaction.
